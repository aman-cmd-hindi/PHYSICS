# Development Phases

## Phase 1 — Foundation
App shell, routing, responsive layout, roles, local persistence, optional authentication and base design system.

## Phase 2 — Course Engine
Chapter/topic model, lesson renderer, progress states, resume position and chapter maps.

## Phase 3 — Question Engine
MCQs, feedback, retries, topic practice, numerical modes and mastery rules.

## Phase 4 — Formula & Visualization
Formula Bank, Formula Labs, animations, diagrams, graphs and reusable simulation engine.

## Phase 5 — PYQ & Testing
Topic/chapter/year PYQs, board-style solutions, chapter/multi-chapter/full tests and adaptive/custom tests.

## Phase 6 — Tutor/Classroom
Tutor mode, Smart Board presentation controls, free navigation, reveal controls and authorized textbook viewer.

## Phase 7 — Revision & Analytics
Spaced repetition, weak-topic recovery, exam-countdown recommendations and simple performance analytics.

## Phase 8 — Offline & Performance
Chapter downloads, caching, offline progress queue, sync reconciliation and performance optimization.

## Phase 9 — Content Production
Verified Maharashtra Board syllabus/content, source ingestion, Content Studio, simulations, questions, PYQs, textbook mapping and review workflow.

## Phase 10 — QA & Launch
Curriculum audit, notation audit, numerical validation, accessibility audit, offline testing, cross-device testing, classroom testing and production release.
